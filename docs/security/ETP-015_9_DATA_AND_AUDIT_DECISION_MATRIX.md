# ETP-015.9 — Data and Audit Decision Matrix

**Status:** `AWAITING HUMAN DECISIONS`

This matrix classifies documented contract categories, never real records. States are limited to
`APPROVED EXISTING POLICY`, `PENDING MATERIAL DECISION`, `BLOCKED`, and `NOT APPLICABLE`.

The existing policy allows deny-by-default, company isolation, allowlist metadata, no automatic
discard before BDP-011, and only the already homologated ETP-015.6 `MINIMAL` projections. It does
not implicitly approve any legacy response field.

For all future audit decisions, `actorId` must come from the authenticated principal and never from
a DTO. Legacy DTOs do not establish an authoritative actor. Employee, contract, admission, leave,
benefit, vacation, variable-compensation, payroll-input, and payroll-run identifiers can directly or
indirectly identify a person and therefore remain within their stated PII/sensitivity blockers.
Metadata and free text may only enter audit through a separately approved allowlist; raw payloads
are never an audit projection.

## Mandatory human decisions

- field allowlist/projection for each legacy operation;
- whether each read is sensitive and must create an audit event;
- event code, atomicity, metadata allowlist, and reason handling for each critical write;
- segregation and full-data capability, if any;
- treatment of free text without logging payloads;
- fields/behaviors that remain omitted while BDP-001/011 or another material BDP is pending.

No masking algorithm, retention duration, export, DLP rule, legal policy, or final LGPD
classification is established here. The audit catalog remains 27 events with 0 approved additions.
